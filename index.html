Over The Horizon: 3D Low-Poly Planetary Driving Odyssey

Over The Horizon is a free-to-play indie 3D low-poly driving simulator and planetary exploration odyssey built with WebGL and Three.js. Players pilot the vintage TrailScout 4x4 Rig overland exploration vehicle across 9 miniature spherical planets orbiting throughout the solar system.

Planetary Destinations

  • Planet Oakhaven (Earth): Lush alpine diorama with towering emerald pine forests, rustic wooden bridges, cascading waterfalls, and drifting cumulus clouds.
  • Planet Cinderfell (Mercury): Scorched volcanic basalt calderas, molten magma streams, and glowing thermal vents.
  • Planet Aetheria (Venus): Sulfuric glasslands, glowing acid sand dunes, and serene twilight atmospheres.
  • Planet Rustvale (Mars): Crimson canyons, ancient dried riverbeds, iron sand dunes, and monumental rock arches.
  • Planet Stormhaven (Jupiter): Tempestuous gas giant peaks, colossal cloud vortexes, and floating ion pylons.
  • Planet Ringhaven (Saturn): Golden ringed horizon, celestial dust fields, and cosmic solar skimmers.
  • Planet Frostveil (Uranus): Azure methane glaciers, steep tilted planetary rings, and sub-zero crystal spires.
  • Planet Thalassa (Neptune): Deep cobalt ocean world, abyssal monoliths, and rolling tidal sub-swells.
  • Planet Nocturna (Pluto): Outer-rim frozen nitrogen plains, dark crystalline peaks, and glittering starlight.

Game Modes

  • Cosy Cruise: Peaceful, relaxing open-trail exploration with dynamic time-of-day transitions, warm sunlight, and scenic vistas.
  • Celestial Cascade: Action-packed celestial survival dodging falling meteorites tracked by glowing ground astrolabes, earning near-miss combo score multipliers.
  • Crossy Odyssey: High-stakes mountain transit dodging rolling boulders, crossing utility convoys, and dynamic traffic hazards across busy planetary arteries.

Vehicle & Controls

Drive the TrailScout 4x4 Rig using keyboard controls [W/A/S/D or Arrow keys] to accelerate, steer, and brake. Press [SPACE] for nitro boost, [C] to cycle between Chase, Free Orbit, and Cockpit camera modes, [H] for auxiliary fog lamps, and [P] for cruise control. Full mobile touch steering, virtual pedals, and accelerometer support available on phones and tablets.

Audio & Soundscapes

Features 100% organic, zero-beep procedural acoustic and analog soundscapes synthesized live in your browser using the Web Audio API. Includes nylon-string acoustic guitars, concert harps, wooden marimbas, kalimbas, Rhodes electric pianos, and warm analog cello swells tailored to each world.
