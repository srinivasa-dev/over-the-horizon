Solitary Indie Project AI-Augmented Engineering

"I always imagined this game."

I've always loved the concept of tiny spherical planets and richly textured low-poly diorama games (inspired by Over the Hill, Art of Rally, and The Little Prince).

Now that we have advanced agentic AI, I wanted to test the frontier: How far can a solitary developer go by vibe coding in pure creative flow with an AI coding partner?

WEBGL GRAPHICS REQUIRED

Your browser or graphics device does not support WebGL or hardware acceleration. Over The Horizon requires WebGL to render miniature planetary worlds.

Please ensure hardware acceleration is enabled in your browser settings or try using the latest version of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge.
