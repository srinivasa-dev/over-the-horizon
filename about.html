The Vision & Creative Spark

I have always loved miniature spherical planets, warm analog diorama landscapes, and tactile overland driving games. The visual inspiration draws from titles like Art of Rally, Over the Hill, and Antoine de Saint-Exupéry's classic novella The Little Prince.

With the advent of advanced agentic AI models like Gemini 3.8 Flash (High) in Google Antigravity, I wanted to explore the frontier of modern software craftsmanship: How far can a single engineer go by vibe coding in pure creative flow with an AI coding partner?

Technical Architecture

Over The Horizon is engineered from the ground up without heavy external game engines, ensuring instant sub-second browser boot times on mobile and desktop:

Three.js WebGL Engine

Procedural spherical planet geometry (R=65m miniature scale), analytical surface-normal gravity, custom low-poly facet lighting, and ACES Filmic tone mapping.

100% Procedural Web Audio API

Zero pre-recorded audio files. Every guitar pluck, harp chime, Rhodes chord, engine harmonic, horn blast, and wind drift is synthesized live mathematically in your browser.

Cloudflare Edge Presence Gateway

Real-time active player counts powered by Cloudflare Workers and SQLite Durable Objects using WebSocket Hibernation, strict Origin security, and silent fail-safe architecture.

Progressive Web App (PWA)

Fully installable on iOS and Android with offline caching via Service Worker, landscape lock, touch controls, and accelerometer tilt steering.
