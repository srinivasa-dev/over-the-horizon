Mission Architectures

Expedition Game Modes

Three tailor-made driving experiences engineered for different player moods: from serene scenic trail cruising to adrenaline-fueled orbital asteroid evasion.

1

Cosy Cruise

Scenic Diorama Trail Exploration • Zero Timers • Zero Stress

Cosy Cruise is built for peaceful open-trail driving and photography. Freely navigate the circuit around the miniature planet, cross wooden bridges, navigate mountain roads, and discover remote outpost cabins with no timers, damage, or fail states.

  • • Dynamic Time & Climate: Adjust the atmospheric slider from dawn to golden hour, midnight starlight, and midday sun.
  • • Autopilot Cruise Control: Press [P] to engage autonomous driving along the road network while enjoying the procedural acoustic guitar soundscape.
  • • Interactive 360° Camera: Drag with mouse or finger to orbit smoothly around your vehicle in Diorama view or switch into Cockpit POV.
2

Celestial Cascade

Orbital Asteroid Evasion • Sacred Geometry Astrolabes • Combo Streaks

Falling stellar meteorites strike the planet from above. Rotating sacred geometry astrolabe reticles appear on the planetary terrain seconds before impact, giving skilled drivers a chance to slalom past incoming strikes.

  • • Close-Call Bonuses: Weave within 2.5 meters of an active impact zone without getting hit to earn +150 Near-Miss points.
  • • Stardust Shards & 5x Combo: Impacted asteroids scatter glittering stardust shards. Collect shards within 4 seconds of each other to ramp your score combo multiplier up to 5x.
  • • Stationary Lock-on Warning: If your rover stays stationary or crawls below 4 km/h for more than 2 seconds, an orbital tracking beacon locks directly onto your coordinates. Keep moving!
  • • 3 Shield Charges: Absorbs hazard hits. Depleted shields can be restored by collecting green field toolboxes along the road.
3

Crossy Odyssey

Mountain Pass Transit • Crossing Convoys • Tumbling Boulders

High-stakes alpine transit through busy planetary arteries. Navigate heavy crossing utility vehicle convoys, railway carts, and tumbling geological boulders rolling down steep slopes.

  • • Crossing Zones: Signal bells and warning signs warn of upcoming crossing utility carts. Thread intersections between traffic gaps for clean transit bonuses.
  • • Rolling Boulders: Massive rock formations break loose and tumble down hillsides. React dynamically with nitro bursts to avoid collisions.
  • • Weave Streaks: String together successive clean crosses without taking damage to achieve high Mission Ranks (Rank S, A, B).
